Weeklies is a weekly meal planner for people who do not want to ask
themselves what to eat every day.

Add the meals you already know how to cook, store the ingredients for each
one, and choose a recipe for every day of the week. Run the same meals over
multiple weeks, change a few days when you feel like it, or rebuild the week
from scratch. The recipe storage is intentionally ingredient-focused: it is
not trying to be a cooking instruction book.

The shopping list scales a planned recipe's ingredients by your portions per
day, merges duplicate lines, and strips preparation notes so "250 g mushrooms
(trimmed)" is bought as "250 g mushrooms".

Everything is stored locally on your device. No account, no ads, no
analytics, no trackers. The app works fully offline; the only network use is
the optional recipe import, which reads recipe data from a page you paste, or
- with your own Mistral API key - asks Mistral to read it for you. The key is
stored on this device only and is never included in backups.

Features:

- Save known recipes with their ingredients
- Plan a recipe for each day of the week; today is highlighted
- Reuse a weekly plan across multiple weeks
- Shopping list with scaling, merging, and check-off
- Import recipes from a URL (JSON-LD, no key needed) or with AI (your key)
- Backup everything to one JSON file and restore it on a new device
- Dark mode, English and French

<b>Privacy</b>: no personal data is collected, stored off the device, or
shared. Ingredient amounts from AI import are normalized to one portion.
